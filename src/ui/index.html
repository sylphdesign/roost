        AI writing in Gmail

        Roost's Gmail can write, refine and summarize email with Claude. Add your own Claude API key (from the Claude Console). Your email text goes to Claude only when you click an AI button. Claude bills your key for each use.

        · Google's own Gemini tools work only in real Gmail: use “Open in Gmail” in the compose window.
