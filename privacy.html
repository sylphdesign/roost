Roost

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Who we are

Roost is a personal desktop app for macOS. It runs only on your own computer. Contact: open an issue on GitHub.

What Google data Roost uses

When you sign in with Google, you can let Roost:

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): read, search, label, archive, draft and send your email inside Roost.
  • Google Calendar (calendar): show your calendars and events, create and change events, answer invitations, and show event reminders.
  • Your email address, to show which account is signed in.

Where your data goes

  • Roost talks directly from your computer to Google's servers. There is no Roost server. Nobody else receives your Gmail or Calendar data.
  • Your Google sign-in tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with the macOS Keychain.
  • Roost does not sell, share, or use your data for ads, and it has no analytics or tracking.
  • Optional AI writing help: only if you add your own Anthropic (Claude) API key and click an AI button, Roost sends the text you are working on (and, for replies and summaries, the email conversation) to Anthropic to create the answer. Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you click an AI button. Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Roost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Sign out of an account in Roost to delete its tokens from your computer. You can also remove Roost's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Roost app and its settings folder removes all data it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
